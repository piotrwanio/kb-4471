1 · parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Tenants that write the day first get their dates flipped. This is the bug #4471 names. Most of the reports are this one. The queued fix belongs to this problem only.

2 · hint: booking-v3 tells people to type month first

Through booking-v3’s form, people at day-first tenants type what the hint prints under the date field, MM/DD/YYYY, and the form reads it their way. The parser is not at fault; the hint is. Intake tagged many of these parser because the symptom looks the same.

Fixing the parser alone makes this worse: once the parser reads day first, people who typed month first because of the hint will be flipped the other way.

A note on one-day moves

Five reports read as a one-day move, not a swap (desk-notes.json): “Tuesday became Monday”, or the same meeting on two different days for two time zones. The desk files them under parser by tenant, and so does this page, so they are counted once. They may be a time zone problem. Check them after the parser fix ships.
